Caching guide

This guide explains how the service caches responses for its clients.

Time to live

Every cached response expires after seventy two hours. Expired entries are purged when the server starts.

The time to live is configured per deployment and never per request.

Invalidation

Entries are invalidated when the underlying document changes. Identical content reuses the stored copy.

Limits

A single response may not exceed twenty five megabytes; larger bodies are rejected before extraction.
